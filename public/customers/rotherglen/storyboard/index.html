R2
Research · Findings
What to keep, and what to fix
Parents value having everything in one place, and teachers find attendance and class email easy. The problems are fragmentation, clicks and mobile.
Keep
✓One login per person, role-aware views. Parents, students and staff each see their own portal.
✓Cycle-day schedules. Rotation, blocks and special days are handled by the system.
✓Clear assignment statuses. To do, In progress, Completed, Graded and Overdue, colour-coded.
✓Official notes and conduct recorded formally and shareable with families.
✓Calendar feeds that parents can subscribe to by child and team.
Fix
1Two systems for one school day. Crescent keeps homework in Schoology with a separate login from the Green Room.
2Information spread across tabs. Progress, conduct, official notes and the contact card are separate stops for a parent.
3Clunky creation for teachers. Assignment setup is called “clunky and at times confounding”, and teachers can't preview the student view.
4Weak on phones. The Assignment Center calendar is day-only on mobile, and families have asked for a parent app.
5Notifications set up piece by piece. Families opt in one event type and one group at a time.
6Forms and team info in odd places. A yellow banner for forms, and a Resources post for team schedules.

“General consensus… they do not like it.” Students on the 2024–25 Assignment Center redesign

One place for the school daySchedule, homework, grades and events in one system and one login.
Today firstEvery persona opens on what needs doing now, not on a menu.
The whole studentGrades, attendance, activities and teacher notes on one profile.
